I’m Dawa Chhiring Sherpa, also known as Fincho. I create 3D environments exploring how light, space, and materials shape a scene. I’m interested in places that feel considered and lived in. Photography lets me notice those qualities in the real world. I’m drawn to quiet details, distinctive light, and strong compositions. The work here reflects both what I create and what I observe.
